# Presenter cue cards

> **Written on Saturday for the earlier 45-second build.** The game is now one round of 6 levels x 30 s with 3 lives, sign-up on the landing page and one leaderboard per workout and mode. The current script is [DEMO.md](../DEMO.md); where this page disagrees with it, DEMO.md is right.

One card per beat of the scripts in DEMO.md. Print one card per A6 page (or index card), **large type**, and hold them at arm's length. Three lines each: what to say, not a speech. Times assume START → results takes about 52 s with the camera (50.0 s measured with the keyboard on `main` d5f2683, plus about 2 s of calibration).

## Judges: 2:40

---

**1 · HOOK · 0:00–0:20**
"Who here skipped a workout this week?"
Home workouts are free. And boring. So we stop.
*(pause, let it land)*

---

**2 · WHAT IT IS · 0:20–0:35**
A workout you play with your whole body.
Just your laptop's webcam.
And a coach that picks your next session.

---

**3 · START · 0:35** *(Operator clicks START)*
It measures your body.
Every move is judged against you.
*(3-2-1 GO)*

---

**4 · THE ROUND · 0:40–1:27**
Call the moves: step · grab · smash · jump · squat.
Point at the silver runner: "my best run, racing me."
Point once at PERFECT. Then let the music play.

---

**5 · RESULTS · 1:27–1:55** *(Player: arms down)*
Every rep counted. Calories, as an estimate.
The coach: what went well, what to try, what next.
Dashboard: your week. Board: per round length.

---

**6 · HOW IT WORKS · 1:55–2:25** *(slide 2)*
Tracking runs in the browser. Video never leaves.
Obstacles are drawn early by each move's measured lag.
Coach: rules today; Claude when a key is set.

---

**7 · CLOSE · 2:25–2:40** *(slide 3, QR)*
"The high score today is ____."
"Who wants to beat it?"
*(hand over, stop talking)*

---

## Notes for the Presenter

- **Words per card** (counted, at about 150 words a minute = 2.5 words a second): judges 1 · 16 words in 20 s, 2 · 20 in 15 s, 3 · 10 in the ~5 s between START and GO (calibration, then the 3 s fly-in), 5 · 24 in 28 s, 6 · 26 in 30 s, 7 · 11 in 15 s. Popular Vote 1 · 15 in 12 s, 3 · 21 in 31 s, 4 · 12 in 15 s, 5 · 8 in 10 s. Every card fits its slot. The round cards (judges 4, Popular Vote 2) are short calls spread over 45 s of play, not a speech.
- **Never say "raise both arms" on the results screen** with the camera: both arms up starts the next round after 1.5 s.
- **Card 6** is the only technical beat; if a judge asks more, hand to the Tech lead (DEMO.md "Likely judge questions").
